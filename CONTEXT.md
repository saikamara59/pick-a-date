# Pick a Date

A date-invite generator: he sends her a link, and the two of them end up with a concrete date (where, what, and when).

## Language

**Invite**:
The link he sends her, carrying everything needed to set up one date.
_Avoid_: Link, card, ticket (the ticket is how a Plan is shown, not the invite itself)

**Plan**:
The finished set of choices for a date: area, neighborhood, a pick for each question, spots, and a day and time. Either person can be the one who makes it.
_Avoid_: Answers, picks, result

**Her-pick invite**:
An Invite where he offers options and she builds the Plan.
_Avoid_: Normal invite, classic mode

**His-plan invite**:
An Invite that already carries his Plan; she only says yes, asks for a Counter-time, or declines.
_Avoid_: Fixed invite, "be there" invite

**Surprise**:
A part of his Plan he has chosen but keeps hidden from her until the date; she sees only that something is planned there.
_Avoid_: Secret, mystery, TBD; never use for Left to him

**Left to him**:
In a Her-pick invite, a question where she hands the choice back to him instead of picking (her button reads "Surprise me").
_Avoid_: Surprise (that is his hidden choice in a His-plan invite)

**Counter-time**:
A different day and time she proposes for a His-plan invite, leaving the rest of his Plan unchanged.
_Avoid_: Reschedule, new time request
