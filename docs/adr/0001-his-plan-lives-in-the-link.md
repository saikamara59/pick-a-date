# His-plan invites carry the Plan in the link, Surprises included

Pick a Date has no database of invites and no way to reach her, so a His-plan invite carries his whole Plan inside the invite link, including the real spot behind every Surprise. Her replies (yes, Counter-time, or change of answer) go to him as emails; he confirms a Counter-time by texting her, and nothing in the app tracks whether a date is "locked in". We accept that a determined person could decode a Surprise from the link rather than adding server-side storage for hidden fields or a confirmation loop.

## Considered Options

- **Store Surprises server-side and reveal them later.** Would keep Surprises truly hidden, but needs per-invite storage, a reveal trigger, and changes the app's "the link is the invite" model for one feature.
- **In-app confirmation of a Counter-time.** He taps Confirm in the email and her link updates. Needs stored invite state and a second round of notifications, and she only sees it if she reopens the link. They are already texting, so the loop adds little.
